Cookie Policy

Last updated: May 22, 2026

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser. They help websites remember things like your preferences and session.

2. How SANGAT uses cookies

We keep cookie use to a minimum. SANGAT Tools uses only what's needed to make the site work:

  • Session cookies — to keep you logged in while you use the tools.
  • Preference cookies — to remember your theme (light/dark) and language choice.

3. Cookies we do not use

We do not use third-party advertising cookies, cross-site tracking, or behavioural profiling. We are not in the business of selling your attention.

4. Third-party services

Some optional features (such as in-page translation) may set their own cookies when you use them. These are controlled by the providers of those services, not by us.

5. Managing cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. Blocking essential cookies may stop parts of the site — such as staying logged in — from working.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this cookie policy as the site evolves. Any changes will appear on this page with an updated date above.

7. Questions

If you have any questions about how we use cookies, please get in touch.
