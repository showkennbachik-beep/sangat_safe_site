Security & Compliance

Your documents are safe with us.

We take security seriously. Our infrastructure and processes are designed to protect your most sensitive documents at every step.

🔐 Encryption Standards

In Transit: All data is encrypted using TLS 1.3
At Rest: AES-256 encryption for all stored files
Processing: Isolated, sandboxed containers per request

🗑️ Auto-Deletion Policy

All uploaded and processed files are automatically and permanently deleted within 1 hour of processing. No copies, no backups, no exceptions.

🏢 Infrastructure

Our servers are hosted on SOC 2 Type II certified cloud infrastructure with 24/7 monitoring, DDoS protection, and automated vulnerability scanning.

📋 Compliance Frameworks

We align our practices with the following standards:

  • GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation)
  • CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act)
  • SOC 2 Type II
  • ISO 27001 (in progress)

🐛 Responsible Disclosure

Found a vulnerability? We run a responsible disclosure program. Report security issues to pdfmy0782@gmail.com and we'll respond within 24 hours.
