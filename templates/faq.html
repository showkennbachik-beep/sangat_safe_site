Frequently Asked Questions

Find detailed answers to common questions below.

General Questions

What is SANGAT Tools?

SANGAT Tools is a comprehensive, enterprise-grade suite of PDF manipulation tools. It allows you to merge, split, compress, convert, and secure your documents directly from your browser with uncompromised speed.

Is it free to use?

Yes! Our core tools are 100% free for basic use with a generous file size limit. If you need higher limits or API access, we offer an affordable Pro tier.

Are there file size limits?

Free users can upload files up to 10 MB per document. Pro users enjoy an expanded limit of 100 MB per file, allowing for massive document processing.

Billing and Payments Questions

How do I upgrade to Pro?

Simply visit our Pricing page, select either the Monthly or Yearly plan, and complete the checkout process. We support Visa, Mastercard, Easypaisa, and JazzCash.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Absolutely. You can cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings. You will retain Pro access until the end of your current billing cycle.

Do you support local payment methods?

Yes, we proudly support Easypaisa and JazzCash for local transactions. The current rate is fixed at 800 Rupees for seamless local upgrades.

Security and Technical Questions

Are my files secure?

Security is our highest priority. All files are processed via 256-bit SSL encryption. We never look at your documents or share them with third parties.

How long are files stored?

For your privacy, all uploaded documents and generated files are automatically and permanently deleted from our servers within 1 hour of processing.

Why did my conversion fail?

Conversions usually fail if the PDF is heavily encrypted or corrupted. Please ensure your PDF is unlocked before uploading, or use our Unlock Tool first.
