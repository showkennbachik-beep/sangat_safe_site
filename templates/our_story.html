Our Story

Why we built a safer way to work with documents.

A note from the founder

SANGAT began with a simple frustration: every "free" PDF tool online wanted your files, your email, and your trust — and gave little back. We wanted something that respected the person on the other side of the screen.

How it started

SANGAT Tools was built around one idea: working with documents should be fast, simple, and genuinely private. We started with a handful of tools — merge, split, compress — and grew it into a full suite of more than thirty document operations, all in one clean interface.
